Who we are

A pre-health home at Georgia Tech.

The Latino Medical Student Association Plus chapter at Georgia Tech is a student-led community for students exploring medicine and the health professions. We connect cultural community with trustworthy pre-health guidance, mentorship, service, and opportunities to grow.

LMSA National unites and empowers current and future health professionals through service, mentorship, and education — with a commitment to improving health in Hispanic, Latino, and other historically marginalized communities.
